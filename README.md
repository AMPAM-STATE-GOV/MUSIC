# M.U.S.I.C.

**Moroccan Universal Supreme Imperial Council** — the international governing body of the
Moroccan State Union.

This repository is the Council's public record: its member states, its instruments, and the
standards member states hold in common. It is documentation. Operational systems, registry data,
and infrastructure are internal and are not mirrored here.

**Platform overview:** [`ampam-platform`](../../ampam-platform) · **Site:** https://ampamgov.org

---

## What the Council is

The Moroccan State Union is a federation of member states operating under a common treaty basis, a
common records standard, and shared infrastructure. M.U.S.I.C. is its governing body.

The Council does not administer member states. Each state governs itself. The Council maintains
what the states hold in common: the treaty instrument they are party to, the standards their
records conform to, the public register of the federation, and the shared platform each state runs
its own government on.

These are the positions the federation is founded on, stated as its own. Nothing here asserts
recognition by, or authority binding on, any government, court, or third party.

## What a member state receives

Accession to the treaty brings a full government stack on shared infrastructure:

- **Government dashboard** — the state's own instance, in its own identity, on common infrastructure.
- **Records and registry** — CER-1880 conformant, with the same control guarantees as every other member.
- **Currency and settlement** — ALDC, common across the federation.
- **Exchange access** — XALD listing for the state's instruments.
- **Communications** — meetings, messaging, and broadcast.
- **Gazette publication** — the state's public notices carried in the federation record.

Optional per-state capabilities are offered separately rather than bundled by default.

Accession is gated. A state does not receive infrastructure on the strength of intent; the treaty
article governing accession is satisfied first, and every step of the process is recorded.

## Common standards

**CER-1880** — the federation's record standard. 5 token types:
identity, instrument, domain, currency, media.
A record issued by any member state is readable and verifiable by every other.

**Control, not claim** — records across the federation are Controllable Electronic Records. Control
transfers through a governed pipeline, never by direct reassignment, and never without an audit
trail. The standard is uniform: a member state's records carry the same guarantees as the founding
state's.

**Three seals** — national thumbprint, state seal, registry seal. Required before issuance.

## The Gazette

The **M.U.S.I.C. Gazette** is the centralized public record of the federation. Member-state notices,
accessions, treaty actions, and published instruments appear there. It is the federation's paper of
record and the canonical place to check whether something was actually published.

---

## Relationship to M.A.G.I.C.

**M.A.G.I.C.** — Moroccan Alliance for Global Intelligence & Cooperation — is the federation's
outward-facing layer: alliance, intelligence, and cooperation with parties outside the Union.

M.U.S.I.C. faces inward and governs what members share. M.A.G.I.C. faces outward. They are separate
bodies with separate mandates.
